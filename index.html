Scam Shield

A practice guide for Filipino seniors. All examples are for learning only — not real messages.

Spot the Scam

Read each sample. Is it a scam or a legitimate message?

Educational Example Only: “You received a ₱1,250 MERALCO refund. Confirm via [link] to credit to your GCash. Expires bukas if not claimed.”

Red flags: Unsolicited link, artificial deadline, and a sender that is not the official GCash shortcode. GCash will never ask you to click a link to claim a refund. Open the app directly.

Educational Example Only: A caller says she is from SSS and sends you a link to “update the SSS app” while you are on the phone. She asks you to stay on the line during installation.

Red flags: Pressure to install during a call, a link that does not go to an official app store, and a request to stay on the line. Government agencies do not ask you to install apps through links.

Educational Example Only: “DSWD ayuda: ₱5,000 for qualified seniors. Confirm today at [link] or it will be forfeited bukas.”

Red flags: A specific peso amount in an unsolicited SMS, a non-government domain, and a same-day deadline. DSWD does not distribute ayuda through text-message links.

Educational Example Only: “Your parcel failed delivery. Pay the ₱50 redelivery fee here: [link].” You were not expecting a parcel.

Red flags: An unexpected parcel, a request for a fee through an SMS link, and an unknown sender. Legitimate couriers do not collect redelivery fees through links.

What To Do If You Suspect a Scam

  1. Stop. Do not reply, do not click, and do not send money.
  2. Do not engage. End the call or close the message.
  3. Contact your bank or e-wallet through the official app or the number printed on your card. Do not use the number from the suspicious message.
  4. Report it. Call the CICC hotline 1326 or report to the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group. Tell a family member or barangay official.

Scam Shield is an educational prototype. All sample messages are fictional and created for practice. For real concerns, contact your bank or CICC 1326.
